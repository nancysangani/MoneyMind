## Metrics Collection Methodology

### Time Tracking
1. **Baseline measurement:** Tracked manual process for 1 month before building MoneyMind
2. **Automated measurement:** Timed each workflow execution
3. **Verification:** Repeated measurements to confirm consistency

### Money Tracking
1. **Subscription audit:** Bank statement analysis of past 12 months
2. **Price comparison:** Manual verification of current INR pricing vs. my rates
3. **Late fee tracking:** Bank statement review of past year

### Validation
- All metrics based on actual usage (not theoretical)
- Time savings measured with stopwatch
- Money savings verified through bank statements
- Dashboard metrics auto-calculated from real PDF data

## How to Track Your Own Metrics

1. **Use the provided CSV files:**
   - `metrics/time_saved_log.csv`
   - `metrics/money_saved_log.csv`

2. **Log each workflow execution:**
   - Date, workflow name, time before, time after, money saved

3. **View on dashboard:**
   - Run `python server.py` → http://localhost:8000
   - All Rs. amounts auto-calculated from your real PDFs
